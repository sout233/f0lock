# Credits font

`NotoSansSC-Credits.ttf` is a subset of Noto Sans SC, instantiated at weight
400 and containing only the Chinese characters and full-width parentheses
used by the credits. It is bundled so contributor names render consistently
without depending on fonts installed on the user's system.

The subset also includes 荔枝 for the contributor name 荔枝fliz.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
